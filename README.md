I am an Assistant Professor at the Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University. My research spans four connected directions, **AI4Cog** (AI for cognitive science), **Cog4AI** (cognitive science for AI), **AI4Brain** (AI for neuroscience) and **Brain4AI** (neuroscience for AI).

**I am looking for collaborators** in AI4Cog, Cog4AI, AI4Brain and Brain4AI. [Email me](mailto:hou.dengzhe.t8@dc.tohoku.ac.jp?subject=Collaboration)

### Selected research

- **[S1CogBench](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/S1CogBench)**, what System One decision models can and cannot do on cognitive experiments ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00911))
- **[CogBattery](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/CogBattery)**, human-referenced evaluation of cognitive task behavior and structure in language models ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24999))
- **[WMIP](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/WMIP)**, a working-memory-inspired programmable probe of cumulative state tracking in language models ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27343))

### Tools and resources

- **[auto-eeg-analysis](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/auto-eeg-analysis)**, EEG analysis skills for MNE-Python, with executable specifications
- **[CogEEGAgent](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/CogEEGAgent)**, bounded cognitive EEG analysis with language models and deterministic scientific controls
- **[Look et al.](https://looketal.com/)**, a design library of academic web pages, each with an AI-readable DESIGN.md
- **[research-writing-prompts](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/research-writing-prompts)**, 科研写作 prompt 合集，25 个场景，逐条标明来源
